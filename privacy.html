What we collect

When you sign in with Google or GitHub, VisionPilot receives your name, email address and profile picture from that account. Nothing else is requested.

Why

  • To keep you signed in.
  • So the owner can see who signed in and approve Advanced mode for an account.

Where it is kept

Your name, email, picture, sign-in provider, sign-in dates and approval status are stored on Cloudflare (Workers KV). Images, settings and prompts you make in the app stay in your own browser and are never uploaded.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with anyone.

Removing your data

Ask the owner to delete your account record by contacting ismailhossainn720@gmail.com. You can also remove VisionPilot's access from your Google or GitHub account settings at any time.
